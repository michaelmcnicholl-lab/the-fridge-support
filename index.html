The Fridge

Little art. Big feelings.

A home for your children's artwork, for parents and children to use together. Photograph a drawing, hear the story behind it if they'd like to tell it, choose a magnet and put it on your family fridge.

What it does

  • Capture. Take a photo or choose one from your library, then crop or straighten it. Your original photo is always kept as it was. Only the picture is needed; the name, date, title and notes are optional.
  • Their story. After adding a picture you can record a short voice memory: "Tell me about your picture." Nothing records until you tap Record, and you can listen back, retake, delete or skip it.
  • On the fridge. Choose a magnet and a spot, by dragging or with the arrow buttons. The fridge shows 12 at a time.
  • Memory Box. Everything you add is kept here, on the fridge or not, limited only by your iPhone's free space. Open any memory to see the whole picture, its date, story and voice, and to edit, share, save it to Photos or delete it.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no login. Your memories are kept on your iPhone and the app works offline.

How do I keep a copy of everything?
Open Settings in the app and choose Save a copy of every memory. You can save the original pictures, voice recordings and a list of their details to Files or anywhere you like. Please do this now and then: deleting the app deletes what is stored on your iPhone.

Is there iCloud backup or a shared family fridge?
The app has early options for these in Settings, but we are still testing them, so please don't rely on them yet. Keep your own copy as above.

How do I take a drawing off the fridge?
Open it and choose Keep in the Memory Box. It is not deleted.

How do I delete a drawing?
Open it and choose Delete, then confirm. This removes the picture and any voice recording from your iPhone.

Can I get a weekly reminder?
Yes, if you want one. Turn on Sunday reminder in Settings. It is off unless you turn it on.
