The Fridge

Little art. Big feelings.

The Fridge keeps your children's drawings, paintings and little masterpieces on a cosy virtual fridge, with a Memory Box for everything you want to keep forever.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no login. Your memories are kept on your iPhone.

How do I back up my drawings?
Open Settings in the app and turn on Back up to iCloud. A private copy is kept in your own iCloud, which only you can see.

How do I share the fridge with my family?
Turn on iCloud backup, then use Family fridge in Settings to invite people. They need The Fridge on their own iPhone.

How do I delete a drawing?
Open the drawing and choose Delete. If iCloud backup is on, the copy in your iCloud is removed too.

How do I remove everything?
Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy as well, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, find The Fridge and delete its data.
