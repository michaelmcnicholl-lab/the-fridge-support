Made for a moment together

"Let's put it on our fridge."

The real fridge fills up fast. Drawings get swapped, curl at the corners and quietly disappear. The story behind each one goes with them.

The Fridge keeps every masterpiece, the artist's name and age, and their own voice explaining what it is. Ten years from now you'll still have the wonky rainbow, and you'll still hear why it had seven suns.

How it works

From the kitchen table to the fridge in seconds

  1. Snap it

    Photograph the drawing or pick it from Photos, then crop and straighten it in a tap.

  2. Pin it

    Pin it to your own cosy kitchen fridge, right where they can see it.

  3. Hear them

    Record your child telling you all about it. Their voice stays with the picture.

  4. Keep it

    Anything you take down from the fridge is kept in the Memory Box, sorted by child.

And a few little extras

Small touches that make it feel like yours

A book of the year

Turn a whole year of drawings into a PDF book, ready to print or send to the grandparents.

Decorate the kitchen

Pick the fridge colour, wallpaper, magnets and stickers. Every family's kitchen looks a little different.

Share the fridge

Make a framed picture of the whole fridge door to send to family, with the option to hide your kitchen's name.

Zoom right in

Pinch the fridge to get up close to every crayon line, just like leaning in at the real thing.

Remember this?

Every so often, the app brings back a drawing from weeks ago so you can smile at it again.

One memory a week

A gentle weekly star for saving a new picture. No streaks to break and no guilt if you miss one.
