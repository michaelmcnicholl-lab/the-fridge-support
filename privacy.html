Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

In short: we don't collect anything. Your family's drawings, photos, voice recordings and names stay on your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud if you choose to turn backup on. We can't see any of it. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores

The Fridge stores the things you add: pictures of artwork, photos, notes, voice recordings, titles, dates and the first names or nicknames of your little artists. All of this is saved on your iPhone.

iCloud backup and family sharing

These options are optional and still being tested, so please also keep your own copy using Save a copy of every memory in the app's Settings.

If you turn on iCloud backup, a copy is saved in your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Text is stored encrypted. This copy belongs to you and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. We, the makers of The Fridge, cannot access it.

If you share your fridge with family members, the people you invite can see and add to your fridge. You can stop sharing at any time in the app's Settings.

Camera, microphone and photos

The app asks to use the camera so you can photograph artwork, and the microphone so you can record your child talking about a drawing. It can save copies to your photo library if you ask it to. These are only used when you choose to, and nothing is sent to us.

Reminders

If you turn on the Sunday reminder, it is scheduled on your iPhone as a local notification. Nothing is sent to us.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Children

The Fridge is made for parents and carers to keep their children's artwork. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete any drawing in the app. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. To delete your iCloud copy, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, find The Fridge and delete its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
